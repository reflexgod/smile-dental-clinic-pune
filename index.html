01 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

A dental studio built for people who usually dread the dentist.

Most dental anxiety comes from feeling rushed, uninformed, or catching the harsh sterile glare of a traditional clinic. When we conceived Smile Dental Clinic for Pune, we started with a simpler question: what helps a person breathe easier the moment they walk in?

It begins before you even arrive—with Riya answering your questions clearly at any hour without pressure. And when you step into the studio, you are met with warm plaster walls, longer appointment buffers, intraoral camera walkthroughs on a screen you can actually understand, and written treatment options before any clinical work begins.

02 / CLINICAL TREATMENTS

Thoughtful care across five core disciplines.

Every treatment plan prioritises tooth preservation, comfort-first anaesthesia, and transparent staging. Select any discipline below or ask Riya how it applies to your situation.

01 · GENERAL & PREVENTIVE DENTISTRY

Foundational care that protects your natural enamel.

Rather than waiting for a toothache, our preventive consultations map enamel wear, gum attachment, and bite balance early. When a restoration is needed, we use biomimetic, tooth-shaded ceramics and composites that bond seamlessly to healthy structure.

What is included

  • Comprehensive oral health & bite evaluation with intraoral photos
  • Low-radiation digital radiographs only when clinically indicated
  • Mercury-free, shade-matched composite restorations (fillings)
  • Single-visit or staged rotary root canal therapy under rubber dam isolation
  • Custom nightguards for clenching and bruxism protection

02 · TEETH CLEANING & GUM THERAPY

Gentle ultrasonic hygiene tailored for sensitive gums.

Routine scaling should never feel rough or scraping. We use fine-tip piezo ultrasonics with warm water irrigation and low-abrasion polishing pastes to lift hardened tartar and tea or coffee staining while keeping exposed root surfaces calm.

What is included

  • Periodontal pocket charting and gum inflammation assessment
  • Gentle piezoelectric ultrasonic scaling above and below the gumline
  • Fine stain removal for coffee, chai, and everyday discolouration
  • Desensitising fluoride or nano-hydroxyapatite enamel conditioning
  • Personalised interdental brush and home-care coaching

Typical visit rhythm

Session Duration
45 minutes (single visit for routine hygiene; staged for deep gum care)
Recommended Cadence
Every 6 months for healthy gums; every 3–4 months for periodontal maintenance

03 · COSMETIC DENTISTRY & SMILE DESIGN

Subtle, natural-looking aesthetics—never artificial or over-prepped.

Great cosmetic work respects natural tooth texture, translucency, and facial proportions. Whether brightening shade by a few natural tones or refining chipped edges with conservative bonding, you preview the proposed shape before committing.

What is included

  • Sensitivity-managed in-studio and take-home enamel whitening
  • Additive micro-layered composite edge bonding (often zero drilling)
  • Ultra-thin, minimal-preparation porcelain veneers crafted with master ceramists
  • Trial smile mockups so you can see and feel changes in natural light
  • Replacement of dark, aging metal-fused crowns with all-ceramic restorations

Typical visit rhythm

Guiding Principle
Least invasive option first—bonding or whitening before ceramic prep

04 · GUIDED DENTAL IMPLANTS

Precision 3D-planned replacements for missing teeth.

Replacing a missing tooth protects neighbouring teeth from drifting and preserves jawbone volume. We combine 3D cone-beam imaging with digital surgical guides so placement is predictable, minimally invasive, and comfortable during recovery.

What is included

  • 3D CBCT bone volume and nerve-pathway assessment
  • Digitally guided biocompatible titaniumimplant placement
  • Custom-milled zirconia and porcelain screw-retained crowns
  • Implant-supported bridges for multiple missing teeth
  • Clear written timeline covering healing phases and maintenance

Typical visit rhythm

06 / COMFORT & TECHNOLOGY

Gentle on the senses. Exact in the details.

Modern dental technology should make appointments quieter, faster, and less invasive. We pair sensory comfort touches with high-precision diagnostics so there is never any guesswork.

Sensory Comfort Details

  • Two-Stage Gentle Numbing We apply a warming topical anaesthetic gel before slow, pressure-controlled local anaesthesia so you barely feel a pinch.
  • Quiet Electric Handpieces Precision electric micro-motors dramatically reduce the high-pitched vibration and sound associated with older air-driven drills.
  • Warm Water Hygiene Our ultrasonic scalers use temperature-regulated water so sensitive teeth are never shocked by cold spray during cleanings.
  • Rest Breaks Anytime Bite cushions for jaw support and unhurried pauses whenever you want to rinse, ask a question, or simply catch your breath.

Diagnostic & Hygiene Standards

  • 3D Intraoral Optical Scanner Replaces uncomfortable gag-inducing putty trays with a wand that builds a full-colour 3D model of your teeth in seconds.
  • Low-Dose Digital Radiography Instant digital sensors reduce radiation exposure compared to conventional film while revealing tiny interproximal changes early.
  • Multi-Stage Autoclave Sterilization Sealed instrument pouches are opened in front of you at the start of every appointment, backed by logged Class-B sterilization cycles.
  • Written Treatment Roadmaps Before any restorative or cosmetic care begins, you receive a clear breakdown of stages, alternatives, and expected timelines.
