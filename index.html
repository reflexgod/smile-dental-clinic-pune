How we approach every appointment at Smile Dental Clinic

Small details that make the chair feel easier.

Being nervous at the dentist usually comes from not knowing what will happen next, or worrying that something will hurt. We structure appointments around four practical habits so you always know where things stand.

We talk before tilting the chair back

You do not start the visit flat on your back under a bright lamp. We spend the first few minutes sitting upright, listening to what has been bothering you and noting any past dental sensitivities.

You see the same photos we see

Instead of asking you to take our word for a hidden cavity or cracked filling, we take close-up camera photos and review them with you on the monitor. We tell you plainly what needs attention soon and what can simply be watched.

Proper numbing and a simple stop signal

We place topical numbing gel on the gum before any local anaesthetic and wait until the tooth is completely numb before starting. If you want to rinse, ask a question, or take a breather, just raise your left hand and we stop right away.

Clear costs before you book a procedure

If you need follow-up work, we give you a written plan showing the appointments required and the exact fee for each step. You can take it home, think it over, and book when you are ready.

What brings you in?

Some patients come in for a six-month scaling and polish. Others book because a molar started aching over the weekend, or because they want to fix a chipped front tooth. Here is how we handle our main areas of care.

Checkups, Scaling & Everyday Care

“It has been a couple of years since my last cleaning, or my gums bleed a little when I brush.”

If you have put off a checkup for a long time, we will not lecture you about flossing. We start by checking your teeth, gums, and existing fillings, then use a warm-water ultrasonic scaler to clear away hardened tartar and tea or coffee staining. If a small cavity needs attention, we repair it with tooth-coloured composite that blends in with your natural enamel.

  • Full tooth and gum examination with intraoral camera photos
  • Ultrasonic scaling and airflow stain polishing
  • Tooth-coloured composite fillings and sensitivity treatment

Whitening, Bonding & Clear Aligners

“I have a chipped front edge, uneven spacing, or yellowed enamel that I want to tidy up naturally.”

Cosmetic dental work looks best when nobody can tell a dentist was involved. Often, small gaps or worn edges can be repaired in a single visit using conservative composite bonding without drilling down healthy tooth structure. For crowded or crooked teeth, we take a 3D optical scan and plan removable clear aligners or braces that move teeth gradually into alignment.

  • In-clinic bleaching and custom take-home whitening trays
  • Conservative composite edge bonding and ceramic veneers
  • Clear aligners and braces planned with 3D digital scans

Toothache Relief, Root Canals & Implants

“A back tooth hurts when I chew, an old crown came loose, or I need to replace a missing tooth.”

When a tooth is keeping you awake, our first job is getting you out of pain. We take a small digital X-ray to find the source of the infection, numb the area thoroughly, and clean out damaged nerve tissue using quiet rotary instruments. Once the tooth settles, we protect it with a custom zirconia or ceramic crown, or plan a dental implant if a tooth cannot be saved.

  • Root canal treatment under magnification
  • Tooth-coloured zirconia and ceramic crowns
  • 3D-guided dental implants and wisdom tooth care

Better tools mean less guesswork in the chair.

A lot of physical discomfort at the dentist comes from outdated equipment, like rubbery impression trays or tiny X-ray films that poke the roof of your mouth. We use digital imaging and hospital-grade sterilisation across every room.

01

Optical scans instead of messy putty

When we make a crown, nightguard, or set of clear aligners, we pass a small camera wand over your teeth for two minutes. It builds an accurate 3D model on screen without a mouthful of setting impression paste.

02

Low-dose digital X-rays on the chairside screen

We only take radiographs when we need to check between teeth or below the gumline. Digital sensors use much lower radiation than film and bring the image up immediately, so your dentist can point out the exact root or filling with you.

03

Sealed instrument pouches for every patient

After each appointment, reusable instruments and handpieces go through ultrasonic cleaning and a Class-B vacuum autoclave cycle. They remain sealed in dated, indicator-stamped pouches until we open them at your chair.

Conservative dentistry, explained in plain language.

Natural tooth enamel is worth keeping. Whenever a small chip, early cavity, or worn filling can be repaired conservatively rather than crowned or extracted, we will tell you so.

General checkups, root canal treatments, crowns, and orthodontic consultations all happen under one roof. Because your clinical notes, photographs, and comfort preferences stay in one record, you do not have to repeat your story each time you visit.
